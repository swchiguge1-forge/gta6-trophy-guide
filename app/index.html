    The road to the platinum

    Four stages, in the order to play them. Finish one stage before the next where the notes say so: the stranger strands and the missable trophies do not all wait for you.

    How the order works

    1. Play the story straight through, chapters 1 to 4 and the finale. Every chapter and named mission trophy pops as you finish it, and the story is the spine everything else hangs on.
    2. Take every stranger strand as its marker appears. Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone each have a chain ending in a final mission, and three more strangers are waiting in Leonida. A strand left unfinished can close once the story moves on.
    3. Do the activities while you are out in the world anyway. Races, the shooting range, fishing, the arcade, bowling, meditation and the challenges all count here - one of each activity type also earns its own trophy.
    4. Clean up after the credits. Collectible cards, WAiNK spots, vehicles, garages, exploring every area of Leonida and 100% completion. Nothing in this stage can be missed, so it is safe to leave it all until last.

    Missable trophies — do these on time

    These trophies are tied to a moment, a person or a choice that may not come back. Everything else in the guide can be earned in free roam after the story, but these reward playing the moment while it is in front of you.
